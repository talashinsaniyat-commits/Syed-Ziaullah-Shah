## 3. Core Systemic Vulnerabilities Identified

1. **Synthetic Reserve Opulence:** Reliance on off-chain commercial paper and non-instantaneous settlement assets to back nominal 1:1 user liabilities.
2. **Proprietary Trading Conflict:** The structural overlap of internal market-making desks and retail order matching engines operating within the same closed database framework.
3. **Withdrawal Bottleneck Latency:** Artificial friction introduced during peak market redemption cycles to protect internal liquidity pools.

## 4. Remediation & Implementation Blueprint (ASF-v1.0 Standards)

To achieve institutional-grade transparency and eliminate structural counterparty risk, the audited architecture must immediately integrate:
* **Continuous Proof of Liabilities (PoL):** Implementation of real-time zero-knowledge accumulator trees that lock internal ledger expansion whenever vault reserves drop below 100% liability coverage.
* **Execution Decoupling:** Total legal and algorithmic separation of proprietary trading desks from retail-facing matching engines.
* **Automated Circuit Breakers:** Trustless smart-contract mechanisms designed to halt systemic leverage cascading during extreme market movements.
